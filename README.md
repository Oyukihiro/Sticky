<h1 align="center">Sticky</h1>

<p align="center">
  Find your Telegram stickers, convert them, and prepare them for WhatsApp.
</p>

Sticky is an Android app that helps you browse stickers from your Telegram account, choose your favorites, and export them as WhatsApp-ready packs. You can also import media files from your device without connecting Telegram.

Supports static and animated stickers, saved GIFs, sticker previews, and WhatsApp Business. Available in English and Portuguese, with light, dark, and AMOLED themes.

> Requires an ARM64 Android device. Telegram sign-in is handled by the official TDLib.

Download the latest version from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Oyukihiro/Sticky/releases).
